Campus Express

Smart campus food marketplace connecting students with vendors.

HTML CSS JavaScript PHP

The Problem

Campus food vendors lack a digital presence, making it hard for students to discover menus, compare options, and order conveniently. Students often waste time walking between vendors or missing out on available options.

No centralized menu system
Manual ordering processes
No vendor discovery tool

The Solution

A platform for vendor discovery, menu browsing, search, reviews, and direct ordering through WhatsApp integration. Campus Express brings the campus food experience to students' fingertips.

Vendor discovery & menus
Search & filtering
WhatsApp ordering integration

Key Features

Smart Search

Find food quickly with intelligent search and filtering options.

Vendor Profiles

Discover vendors with menus, ratings, and location information.

Reviews & Ratings

Read reviews and rate vendors based on food quality and service.

WhatsApp Ordering

Order directly through WhatsApp for a seamless experience.

Technology Stack

HTML5
CSS3

Project Status

Functional prototype with vendor listings and ordering workflow. The platform demonstrates a complete food discovery and ordering experience for campus students.
